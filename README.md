# MONY 📊

**Indian Equity Market Intelligence Platform**

> MONY helps people understand the market. It does not tell them what to buy or sell.

---

## The Most Important Rule

**MONY must never tell users which stock to buy or sell.**

MONY provides:
- ✅ Verified market facts
- ✅ Historical observations
- ✅ Technical indicator values and explanations
- ✅ Relevant news and source links
- ✅ Possible interpretations — clearly labelled as interpretations
- ✅ Supporting and contradicting evidence
- ✅ Risk calculations and limitations
- ✅ Educational explanations

MONY never provides:
- ❌ "Buy this stock now"
- ❌ "This stock will definitely rise"
- ❌ Guaranteed profit claims
- ❌ Certain market predictions

## Product Scope

**In scope:**
- Indian listed cash-equity shares
- Intraday market research
- Equity market scanning and exploration
- Global financial context relevant to Indian equities
- Financial news and company announcements
- Technical analysis
- Historical market analysis
- Trading education
- Risk and position-size calculations
- Backtesting and historical strategy research
- Simulated paper trading
- Personal research notes and trading journals

**Out of scope:**
- Futures and options / derivatives
- Long-term investing or portfolio management
- Brokerage services or real-money order execution
- Guaranteed-return claims
- Automated trading

## Intelligence Principles

1. Evidence before explanation
2. Explanation before interpretation
3. Uncertainty must be visible
4. Every market claim traceable to a source or calculation
5. Historical relationships ≠ proof of causation
6. Technical indicators are measurements, not guarantees
7. Market coverage and data freshness must be explicit
8. Willing to say evidence is insufficient
9. No fabricated signals or manufactured certainty
10. User education > trading frequency
11. Every financial calculation must be deterministic and tested
12. Strategy performance evaluated after all costs
13. Important results must be reproducible
14. Financial accuracy is non-negotiable

## Definition of Success

MONY succeeds when users can:

- Understand financial and market concepts more accurately
- Investigate price movements using evidence
- Distinguish facts from speculation
- Recognise uncertainty and analytical limitations
- Understand transaction costs and risk
- Learn from historical examples and counterexamples
- Evaluate strategies using reproducible methods
- Maintain useful research and simulation records
- Make more informed independent decisions
